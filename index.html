How It Works

Finding businesses near you

AreaPlug makes it simple to discover and connect with businesses right in your area.

1
Pick your plug
Select the plug for your campus or region. FuoyePlug for FUOYE students, and more plugs coming soon.
2
Browse or search
Scroll through businesses or use the search bar to find exactly what you're looking for by name or product.
3
Filter by what matters
Narrow down by category, location, campus, delivery, or physical store to find the right business fast.
4
Connect directly
Click any business to see what they offer, then hit Visit Business to go straight to their WhatsApp or page.

For Business Owners

Get listed in minutes

It takes less than 5 minutes to get your business in front of thousands of students.

1
Pick your plan
Choose a tier. Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum. Higher tiers get better placement on the platform.
2
Choose your duration
Select how many months you want to stay listed. Pay for 12 months and get 40% off.
3
Fill in your details
Add your business name, category, location, a photo, and a list of what you sell or offer.
4
Pay and go live
Pay securely via Paystack and your business goes live on the platform immediately after.
